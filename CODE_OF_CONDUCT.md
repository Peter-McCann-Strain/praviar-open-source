# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Helpful participation includes empathy, constructive technical criticism,
respect for different viewpoints, acknowledgement of mistakes, and attention to
the project's safety and research limitations.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention,
personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained
disruption, or pressuring anyone to reveal confidential legal, scientific, or
security material.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behaviour is unsafe or
inappropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the private contact
method on their GitHub profile. Do not post personal details publicly. Reports
will be reviewed in good faith with as much confidentiality as practical; no
response deadline or specific outcome is guaranteed.

This policy is informed by the
