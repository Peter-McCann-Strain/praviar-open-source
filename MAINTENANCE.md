# Maintenance policy

This repository is maintained on a best-effort basis as an engineering portfolio
and research preview. Maintenance capacity may vary and no response deadline is
promised.

There is no supported release line or compatibility window. Fixes may be made
to the default branch when practical; archived commits receive no routine
backports.

Changes are accepted when they preserve tenant isolation, fail-closed evidence
handling, deterministic tests, explicit configuration, and the project's
research-preview boundary. Features that imply legal clearance or unsupported
accuracy claims will not be accepted.

The maintainer may later mark the repository read-only without providing a
final supported version.
